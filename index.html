Welcome to our Virtual First Friday gallery! Inspired by Richmond's vibrant art walk, this space celebrates the innovative work of students in creative technology courses. Explore the current showcase or dive into our archives.

CREA 202: Creative Coding & Music Production

Students in this course use Python with EarSketch to create music through code, learning programming fundamentals while exploring algorithmic composition and sound design.

CREA 330: Interdisciplinary Web Design

This course focuses on modern web development using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Students design and build responsive, interactive websites for various purposes, from portfolios to e-commerce mockups.

CREA 391: 2D Game Design & Development

Students use GameMaker to bring their 2D game ideas to life. The curriculum covers game mechanics, level design, sprite animation, and scripting, culminating in a playable game prototype.
